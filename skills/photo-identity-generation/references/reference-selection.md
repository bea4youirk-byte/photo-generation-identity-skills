# Reference selection

## Identity references

Prefer 3–8 clear photographs that collectively show:

- frontal face at eye level;
- left and right three-quarter views;
- profile when the target scene uses a profile;
- neutral expression and one natural smile;
- natural daylight or diffuse indoor light;
- no sunglasses, beauty filters, heavy retouching, extreme makeup, or wide-angle distortion;
- face large enough to inspect eyes, nose, lips, jaw, and hairline.

Reject or down-weight distant full-body images, screenshots with compression, occluded faces, extreme lenses, and older photos that conflict with current appearance.

## Body references

Use clear full-body front and side views with normal perspective. Record only observable proportions; do not infer measurements that are not visible.

## Scene reference

Use exactly one scene reference per output. It may control:

- pose and hand placement;
- outfit and fabric behavior;
- subject scale and crop;
- lens feel and camera angle;
- background geometry;
- light direction, shadows, and color mood.

It must not control the target person's face, age, body type, hair color, or permanent features.

## Difficult cases

A small face in a full-body frame, closed eyes, strong head rotation, hard sunlight, motion blur, and occlusion reduce identity reliability. Use a canonical anchor or an intermediate portrait when accuracy is more important than speed.
